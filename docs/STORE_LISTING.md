# Store listing

**Name:** Notchly
**Short description:** A living Dynamic Island for Windows — music, timers, pop-ups and more, at the top of your screen.
**Price:** Free

## Description

Notchly turns the top of your screen into a true-black notch that shows what's happening right
now and opens into a small control centre when you hover it.

- Music from any player, with synced karaoke lyrics
- Beautiful pop-ups for volume, brightness, battery, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Caps Lock and language
- Focus timer, water reminders, to-dos you type naturally, a file shelf and clipboard history
- Ask: AI that runs on your own PC with Ollama (or your own key), plus translate and quick maths
- Weather, calendar, prayer times, Nepali date, world clocks and desktop widgets
- Privacy dots that show when an app uses your mic, camera or location
- Live Activities: your builds, downloads and scripts can post to the notch
- Smooth spring animations at your display's refresh rate, 0 % CPU when idle, ~2 MB

Private by default: no account, no tracking, every online feature can be turned off.
Everything is free.
